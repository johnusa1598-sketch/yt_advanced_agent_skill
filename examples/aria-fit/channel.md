2026. It shows what a filled-in channel profile looks like. Replace
every number with your own channel's numbers before using it.

- Niche in one sentence: AI-generated gymnastics and flexibility
  Shorts with one recurring character, Aria.

## Character spec, locked

- Hair: curly dark hair in a big messy bun.
- Face: thin GOLD wire-frame glasses, SMALL silver
  hoop earrings, light thin freckles. No nose ring, no septum ring, no nostril jewellery.
- Outfit: leotard rotation, in fixed cycle order — purple
  (anchor, Slot 1), teal, black, royal blue, burgundy, then
  back to purple. One planned slot per video; the hook frame
  and the video share the slot; never the same colour twice
  in a row; never a colour change mid-shot. Example log:
  last slot teal (arabesque, Part 5). See `/yt-ai-prompt`
  rotation and `/yt-series` for the log.
- What this character never does: claims a real performance that
  did not happen, or hides the AI disclosure.

## What this channel never does

  demonstrated.
- Long videos: none at audit time, which is the gap the next
