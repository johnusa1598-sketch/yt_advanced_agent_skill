# Series bible: 30-Day Splits (Aria Fit)

## The promise

> One flexibility step a day, until Aria holds a full split.

## The arc

| Season | Episodes | Where it ends (the finale) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 30 daily Shorts | A clean, held front split, filmed in one take |

## The episode shape

Fixed beats:

1. The day's step, named on screen in the first second.
2. One attempt, one correction, one clean rep.
3. Last 3 seconds: tomorrow's step, spoken and captioned.

What varies (in substance): the stretch, the difficulty, and
the correction each day - not just the intro and outro.

Casting: Mia is the default co-star. Duo is the default;
solo episodes are the exception, capped at 2–3 per week,
and on a solo slot CHEERS must still be witnessed somehow
(see `/yt-story`).

## Routing

| Episode | Related Video target | Last-3-seconds pointer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Day 1 | "Full splits tutorial" (long-form) | "Tomorrow: the hip opener that makes day 2 possible" |

Shorts have no end screens. Related Video is the only in-player
route from a Short to a long video.

## Fallback playlist

If Shorts Series is not available in Studio yet, the 30 episodes
run as a playlist in day order, linked from every episode's
pinned comment.
