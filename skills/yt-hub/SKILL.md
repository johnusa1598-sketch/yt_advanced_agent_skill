# yt-hub

This skill does not write the video. It picks the skill that
does, and it keeps the workflow in order.

## Routing

- An AI video prompt, a character that keeps changing, or a
  prompt that must be hard to copy: `/yt-ai-prompt`.
- AI video production in two steps (hook-frame image, then
  video from that frame), reference discipline, or the
  forbidden three: `/yt-production`.
- A story spine for a Short — Story DNA, parasocial beat,
  eye-contact rule, loop bridge, comment trigger:
  `/yt-story`.
- Glitch prevention — skill-safety ladder, banned combos,
  face-visibility lock, duo separation, glitch burst rule:
  `/yt-anti-glitch`.
- Scoring a finished video before it ships — segment method,
  beat verification, face-crop QC, ten-point scorecard, face
  gate: `/yt-qa`.
- A script, a hook, or an opening line: `/yt-script`.
- A title, a thumbnail, or a CTR problem: `/yt-package`.
- How the algorithm ranks a video, or why one flopped:
  `/yt-algorithm`, before you repackage it.
- Hashtags, keywords, or what is trending: `/yt-trends`.
- The whole publish package at once - titles, description,
  tags, thumbnail brief, text layers, timing, pinned comment,
  A/B plan: `/yt-post`.
- Background music, sound design, or a loop point:
  `/yt-sound`. A meme slot, or a funnier Short: `/yt-meme`.
- A video that flopped, or numbers you need read properly:
  `/yt-diagnose`, which reads the Studio export gate by gate.
- Originality, AI disclosure, or safety before publishing:
  `/yt-originality`, before `/yt-publish`, always.
- Turning Shorts views into long-form watch time, or a
  Shorts Series: `/yt-bridge`, with its series bible.
- Testing a thumbnail or title, or changing a Short's cover:
  `/yt-test` - native Test & Compare for long-form, the manual
  protocol for Shorts.
- A pinned comment, a poll, or a community post:
  `/yt-community` - drafts only, never automation.
- Monetisation, YPP thresholds, or the February 2027 bar:
  `/yt-monetize`, with the runway planner.
- Captions to check, or an audio licence to log:
  `/yt-sound`, extended with the SRT QA script and licence log.
- One idea taken all the way to publish, or a retention curve
  planned end to end: `/yt-viral-loop`, which chains the rest.
- A Short, a daily Shorts plan, or a Shorts-only channel:
  `/yt-shorts-first`, then `/yt-package`.
- A series, a recurring character, or a repeat idea:
  `/yt-series`.
- Numbers, growth, or "why is growth slow": `/yt-analytics`,
  then `/yt-audit` for the single fix.
- A transcript to cut: `/yt-edit`. A long video to clip:
  `/yt-shorts`. Chapters: `/yt-chapters`. Retention:
  `/yt-retention`. What is working in a niche: `/yt-viral`.
- A description, tags, or search: `/yt-seo`. Comments:
  `/yt-comment`. A week or month plan: `/yt-plan`.
- A video that is finished: `/yt-publish`, last, always.

## The workflow for one video

1. Idea and format: `/yt-plan` or `/yt-series`.
2. Anti-glitch first, when the video is AI generated:
   `/yt-anti-glitch` — title-first, skill on the ladder,
   banned check, face and separation checks.
3. Production, when the video is AI generated:
   `/yt-production` — the two-step pipeline (hook-frame image,
   then video from that frame) with reference discipline —
   and `/yt-ai-prompt`, with the character lock, the realism
   core, and the negative prompt.
4. Story spine: `/yt-story` — the eight Story DNA elements in
   arc order, the parasocial beat, the eye rule, the loop
   mode, the comment trigger.
5. Script and hook: `/yt-script`.
6. Algorithm pass: `/yt-algorithm` - one prediction to test.
7. Trends: `/yt-trends` - the hashtag and keyword pack.
8. Sound and meme slots: `/yt-sound`, then `/yt-meme`.
9. The full package: `/yt-post`, with the retention plan from
   `/yt-viral-loop`.
10. Description and search: `/yt-seo`.
11. QA gate for AI video: `/yt-qa` — segment list checked beat
    by beat, face-crop verdict, scorecard against the publish
    threshold. A FAIL stops the chain here, not at publish.
12. Originality, disclosure, and safety gate: `/yt-originality`,
    before anything goes out.
13. Publish check: `/yt-publish`, last, always.
14. After it is live: comments with `/yt-comment`, retention
    with `/yt-retention`, numbers with `/yt-analytics`, and
    diagnosis with `/yt-diagnose` - and the one fix feeds the
    next brief.
15. Between uploads: community with `/yt-community`, the bridge
    with `/yt-bridge`, and tests logged with `/yt-test`.

Or start at `/yt-viral-loop` and let it run the whole chain.

## Rules

- Read the channel profile and the voice file first, when they
  exist. Do not invent numbers, results, or sources.
- Hand back one copy-paste block per step, and end every run
  with the question: **ship it, or change it?**
- Nothing here publishes. This skill writes and you publish.
