# Community routine: drafts for manual posting only

Nothing here posts, replies, or comments for you. Draft, then
post by hand.

## Pinned-comment bank (rotate formats; 1-2 lines each)

- Challenge: "[Beat this: / Your turn: / Can you ...?]"
- Debate: "[A or B? / Which one counts: ...?]"
- Question: "[What should Aria try next: ...?]"
- Route: "[Full progression here: / Next episode drops ...]"

Per video: draft during scripting, pin within 5-30 minutes of
publishing, stay in the thread 30-60 minutes.

## Poll bank (tied to a real upcoming video)

| Poll question | Answers | Decides which video | Result reported in |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [e.g. Which skill next: splits or backbend?] | [A / B] | [episode title] | [episode title] |

A poll whose result is never mentioned teaches viewers their
vote did not matter.

## Post calendar (80/20 value to promotion)

| Gap before next upload | Post | Type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [date] | [teach / ask / show something] | value |
| [date] | [new video notice] | promo |

Since September 2026, community posts can be recommended in
the Shorts feed on web and mobile - the calendar is part of
the Shorts workflow, not an extra.
